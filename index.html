GOOD TO KNOW

A few quick
answers.

Can customers really buy items here?

Not yet. This starter demonstrates shopping-cart features, but it does not collect money, create real orders, or send customer information to a store owner.

Can I upload my own products?

Yes. Edit the PRODUCTS list in app.js and replace the illustrations inside the assets folder with your own product photos.

Can I connect real payment methods later?

Yes, with a secure e-commerce solution or payment provider. A published front-end alone is not a safe checkout system.
